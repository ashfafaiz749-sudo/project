Welcome to Dubai

Dubai is a beautiful city in the United Arab Emirates. It is famous for its tall buildings, beautiful beaches, shopping malls, desert experiences and amazing attractions. Discover the wonderful places of Dubai with AirRite Travels.

Famous Places to Visit in Dubai

Explore the most beautiful attractions in Dubai.

~Burj Khalifa~

Visit the world's tallest building and enjoy amazing views of the Dubai skyline.

~Museum of the Future~

Discover a beautiful building and explore exciting ideas about science and technology.

~Dubai Frame~

Enjoy wonderful views of old and modern Dubai from this famous landmark.

~Palm Jumeirah~

Explore the famous palm-shaped island, beautiful beaches and waterfront views.

~Dubai Fountain~

Enjoy the spectacular water, light and music show near Burj Khalifa.

~Dubai Desert~

Experience the beautiful golden desert scenery and traditional desert activities.
